## Things met on the way

- **A keyword in quotes is a mention.** RFC 8174 lists "MUST", "SHOULD" ... in quotes; without
  that rule its boilerplate read as a prohibition.
- **Earley terminals match one way.** `BREAKING CHANGE` and an ordinary trailer key share a
  prefix; as alternatives of one terminal the short reading won and the trailer was lost.
- **YAML reads commas inside `{...}`.** A meaning with a comma has to be quoted.
- **The translate extra is heavy**: argostranslate needs stanza, stanza needs torch, and torch
  arrives with its CUDA libraries - about 5 GB in the environment. It is an extra for that
  reason; `task install` brings it because it installs every extra.
- **Private or public cannot be seen from a clone.** `analyze` asks GitHub once per
  repository; no answer counts as private.

## The time path (question 4, for later)

From the two blog articles the missing piece is defined well enough to build when wanted: a
moment is the number of milliseconds from the Unix epoch as 32 hexadecimal digits, with a
direction - `UNIXUSAT` after the epoch, `OFCHRIST` before it (counting backwards) - and as
a path the digits fall into eight groups of four:

    UNIXUSAT/0000/0000/0000/0000/0000/019C/DDDB/B8C1      2026-03-11
    OFCHRIST/0000/0000/0000/0000/0000/371B/0BC4/0380      new year of the year 50

The devlog's own names already use it (`scrapnote-<UNIXUSAT in decimal>`). Skipped for now,
as decided; `data-heuristicality/code-analysis/` stays as it is.
